In the expanse of stillness, where judgment dissolves, reality reveals itself.

In the silence, we were found

Sitting in my car in Delhi in 2007, I couldn't have known what life was quietly setting in motion. The years that followed unfolded in ways I could never have imagined — sixteen years as an investor, a daughter who changed my world, travels across eighteen countries, and the grace of a guru who peeled away untruth layer by layer.

What started with an ambition, was fulfilled by grace. I did not set out to seek the absolute; it simply kept unfolding through every decision, every cycle and moments of quiet reflection.

Even in my work, the same truth kept revealing itself — that clarity comes not from noise, but from stillness; not from prediction, but from observation. Over the years, markets became mirrors, teaching patience, humility, and the courage to see things as they are. Patterns emerged when I stopped trying to force them, clarity arrived when I stepped back.

Somewhere along the way, I realized I wasn’t the one seeking truth — truth was seeking me, using every rise and fall to unravel the illusions I held onto. And so I walk this path — not as the doer, but as one being shaped by what is true.

In being, we offer

This space is simply an open door — a humble giving back of everything I have been trusted with. Inside, you will find the footprints of my travels, my quiet adventures within the markets, and the privilege of parenting — of getting to look at the world through an innocent lens once again.

I do not claim ownership over these reflections. I have simply learned to sit still enough to let them pass through me clearly. The perspective here will likely feel unconventional. We live under a system that demands constant motion, blindly rewarding frantic activity over actual, meaningful results. This space is a quiet antidote to that noise.

It is an invitation to step out of the rush, to look at the world with absolute clarity, and to trust that from stillness, everything grows.
